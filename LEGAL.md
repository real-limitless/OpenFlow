## Clean-room development practice

OpenFlow’s node and engine work is organized around a **clean-room process**:

1. Behavioral **specs** are written from **public documentation** and publicly documented formats (not from third-party engine source trees).
2. Specs may be refined across multiple AI-assisted iterations.
3. **Implementation** is produced from those specs and the OpenFlow Plugin SDK as original project code.

This is a **development practice** intended to avoid copying third-party source. It is **not** a legal warranty, certification, or guarantee of non-infringement. For legal advice, consult your own counsel.
